# GMT Scripts — Geophysical and Geological Mapping of the Bolivian Andes

GMT (Generic Mapping Tools) shell scripts used to produce the figures in the peer-reviewed article by Polina Lemenkova. The scripts integrate and map geophysical and geological variables of the Bolivian Andes: topography, geoid undulations, free-air gravity anomaly and its gradient, and seismicity.

**Published in:** *Data* **2022**, *7*(6), 74
**DOI:** https://doi.org/10.3390/data7060074
**Journal (open access):** https://www.mdpi.com/2306-5729/7/6/74
**HAL:** https://hal.science/hal-03684405
**Zenodo:** https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.6602398
**SSRN:** https://ssrn.com/abstract=4125163

## Contents
GMT shell scripts for topographic mapping, geoid undulations (EGM2008), free-air gravity anomaly and its gradient/curvature, and seismicity (earthquake epicentres by depth and magnitude), using GEBCO/SRTM relief grids (grdimage, grdcontour, makecpt, pscoast, psxy, psscale, psbasemap, pstext, psconvert).
